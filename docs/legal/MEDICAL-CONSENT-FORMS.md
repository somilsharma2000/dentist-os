# Dentist OS — medical and treatment consent form pack

**Draft operational forms — treating dentist and Indian counsel must approve the final language. Dentist OS is not the treating provider.**

## A. Patient registration and history acknowledgement
I confirm that the information I provided about my identity, contact details, medical history, medicines, allergies, pregnancy status, and dental history is accurate to the best of my knowledge. I will tell the clinic if it changes.

Patient/guardian: __________ Date: ______ Staff witness: ______

## B. Examination and treatment consent
The dentist has explained the proposed examination/treatment, reasonable alternatives, material risks, expected benefits, costs, and the option to ask questions or refuse. I understand that outcomes cannot be guaranteed and that urgent symptoms should be reported promptly.

Treatment: __________ Dentist: __________ Patient/guardian: __________ Date: ______

## C. Imaging, photographs, and records
Select separately: [ ] diagnostic imaging  [ ] clinical photographs  [ ] before/after educational use  [ ] sharing records with another provider on request. The clinic must document purpose, audience, withdrawal process, and retention.

## D. Minor / guardian consent
I confirm that I am the parent or legally authorised guardian of the minor and have authority to consent to the stated examination/treatment. Relationship and verification: __________

## E. Teleconsultation consent (only if offered)
I understand the limitations of remote assessment, the possibility that an in-person examination is required, privacy risks, emergency limitations, and the identity of the treating practitioner.

## F. Financial and cancellation acknowledgement
The clinic should complete its lawyer/CA-approved estimate, cancellation, refund, tax, and payment terms here. This acknowledgement is separate from clinical consent.

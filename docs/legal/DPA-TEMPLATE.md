# Dentist OS — Clinic Data Processing Agreement template

**Draft only — Indian counsel must review and complete before signature.**

This agreement is between **[Dentist OS legal entity]**, located at **[address]** (Service Provider), and **[Clinic legal entity]**, located at **[address]** (Clinic).

## 1. Roles and instructions
The Clinic determines why and how patient and clinic data is processed. Dentist OS processes data only to provide the subscribed software, hosting, support, backups, messaging integrations, security, and agreed operational services.

## 2. Data categories
Patient identity/contact data, appointment data, dental/health data entered by the Clinic, invoices, consent and opt-out records, support messages, account data, audit/security logs, and integration delivery metadata.

## 3. Confidentiality and access
Dentist OS will restrict access to authorised personnel with confidentiality obligations, least privilege, tenant separation, authentication, logging, and access removal on departure.

## 4. Security and incidents
Dentist OS will maintain documented safeguards and notify the Clinic without undue delay after confirming a security incident affecting the Clinic data. The parties will cooperate on containment, evidence, patient/regulator communications, and legally required reports.

## 5. Subprocessors and transfers
Current subprocessors, purpose, location, and data categories are listed in **[subprocessor register URL/version]**. Dentist OS will provide notice of material additions or changes as agreed. Cross-border processing terms must be completed by counsel.

## 6. Patient rights support
Dentist OS will provide the Clinic with access to export, correction, consent, grievance, and deletion-request records. The Clinic decides whether a request is legally permitted and remains responsible for identity verification and patient communication.

## 7. Retention and deletion
At termination, Dentist OS will return or delete Clinic data according to the Clinic’s written instructions and the agreed retention schedule, except data that must be retained by law or for documented security/legal claims.

## 8. Commercial and liability terms
Complete fees, service levels, warranties, indemnities, insurance, liability limits, governing law, dispute resolution, and termination assistance in the signed commercial agreement.
